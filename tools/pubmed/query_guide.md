Query guide v1

Generate review-level disease and parameter concepts with true synonyms and historical names. MeSH vocabulary proposed by a model is candidate vocabulary until checked against the controlled vocabulary; do not describe it as verified. Keep concepts separate and field-tagged, retain the declared publication window, and never add known included-study titles, authors, identifiers or reference pooled answers. Remote query translation and warnings are actual execution evidence, not proof of search sensitivity. Preserve the executed query, IDs, retrieval date and completeness status. A limited or failed export is not a complete candidate pool. Query generation is upstream of fixed-pool screening/coding/synthesis evaluation and must not modify the frozen evaluation pool.
