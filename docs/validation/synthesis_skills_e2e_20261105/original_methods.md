# Original review method (fictional source, used only to validate the agent path)

## Pooling

Study-level mean incubation-period estimates were pooled by the inverse-variance
method with a random-effects model, using the DerSimonian-Laird estimator for
between-study variance, and reported with a 95% confidence interval.

## Selection

One estimate per independent study cohort was retained. When a study reported
more than one cohort, the cohort with the largest reported sample size was
used, and the other cohorts were excluded.

## Units

Estimates reported in hours were converted to days by dividing the point
estimate and its standard error by 24. The conversion was recorded for every
affected estimate.

## Estimates not pooled

Estimates reported as a median or as an interquartile range were not pooled
with mean estimates, because they describe a different statistic of the
incubation-period distribution.
