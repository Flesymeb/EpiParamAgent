# Reproduction numbers

- Apply the complete research question and eligibility criteria. The basic reproduction number (R0) concerns transmission in a susceptible population under its stated conditions; time-varying/effective reproduction numbers (Rt/Re) concern the conditions and susceptible population at the specified time. Preserve the source definition; notation alone is not decisive.
- Epidemic growth rates, doubling times, secondary attack proportions, and outbreak forecasts are not themselves reproduction numbers. They may support an eligible estimation method when the task permits it.
- A model fitted to observed incidence, surveillance, contact, or genomic data can produce an original estimate. Aggregate data or re-analysis of shared observations does not automatically make the study ineligible.
- Separate an estimated model output from a value only cited or supplied as a fixed model input. Model type or transmission terminology alone does not establish a relevant estimate.
- At title/abstract screening, a task-supported estimation path can require full-text verification without an explicit R0/Rt/Re value. At full text, inspect methods, results, tables, and supplements for the eligible estimate and definition.
- Retaining raw data for new estimation, or a secondary synthesis, requires the task to allow that evidence form. Do not widen the scope or substitute Rt/Re for an R0-only target.
