# Transmission and disease intervals

- Apply the complete research question and eligibility criteria; a multi-parameter task retains every allowed target.
- Serial interval is the time between symptom onset in linked infector and infectee cases. Generation interval/time is the time between their infections. Incubation period is the time from infection to symptom onset. Latent period is the time from infection to becoming infectious. These intervals are not interchangeable.
- Check the stated start and end events, linked-case evidence, observation windows, and estimation method. Original fitted-model estimates can be eligible; individual-level data are not a universal requirement.
- At title/abstract screening, relevant linked-case, exposure/onset, or interval-estimation evidence can warrant full-text verification without an explicit value. Broad disease or transmission language alone is insufficient.
- At full text, distinguish a target interval estimated or reported by the study from a cited value or fixed model input. Missing supplements remain a source gap, not proof that no estimate exists.
- Raw timing data requiring calculation or refitting, and secondary synthesis, qualify only when the task permits that evidence form. Do not substitute a different interval or invent an unreported estimate.
