Preserve uncertainty label, level, bounds and scale independently. CI, CrI, SD,
SE, IQR and range are distinct. Do not infer 95% from convention. A missing
interval does not erase a reported point estimate. Missing is not an observed zero.
Source labels, original values/units, statistical method and case definitions
remain source-grounded; do not use typical disease ranges or testing answers.

If the schema includes distribution, it describes a fitted/modelled distribution
of the target epidemiological outcome, not the sampling distribution of its
estimator. Inspect Methods, Results and relevant table/figure captions for an
explicit family and its link to this outcome/model. Supply an exact contextual
field quotation when a family is reported; keep source-supported families even
when the point estimate is reported elsewhere. A normal approximation used for
a CI, standard error, test or meta-analysis is not evidence that the underlying
outcome distribution is normal. Do not infer a family from a CI, mean/SD pair,
skewness, negative values, a method name alone or common practice. When no family
is explicitly supported in the supplied material, use null (or NR only for a
required string field); this is not verified absence from an unseen document.
