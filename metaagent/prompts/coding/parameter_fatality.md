Keep the source mortality endpoint and case definition explicit. CFR/IFR,
hospital/ICU/IMV fatality, person-time mortality, OR/RR/HR are not interchangeable.
Cases are not automatically laboratory-confirmed; ventilated does not automatically
mean invasive ventilation. Match deaths and denominators within the same population,
time and follow-up. Count-only records retain null unreported rates/intervals.
Never infer zero from silence or derive a rate/CI or missing count. Preserve
original rate units; code performs authorized transformations with provenance.
