Screening tool guide v1

Apply the complete declared eligibility protocol to the supplied source. A downstream synthesis estimand does not narrow whole-review eligibility. Retain, exclude and unresolved review are different outcomes. Missing abstracts, missing keywords and uncertain publication types are not proof of ineligibility. Evidence quotations must be exact source substrings. R0, Rt/Re, serial interval, generation time, incubation period, death counts and fatality proportions have distinct meanings; report ambiguity rather than silently substituting one. Publication-type metadata may conflict with original cohort or surveillance data: inspect content. Do not infer absence from incomplete or budget-limited full text. Duplicate candidates are navigation hints; retain their IDs and assess their source identity. Never use reference labels, pooled SR answers or benchmark membership as scientific evidence.
